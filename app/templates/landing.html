What it does

It's a private, AI-powered health log that lives entirely inside Telegram. You tell it what happened — a workout, a meal, your weight — and it stores it. Ask it a question about your own data and it answers using your actual history, not guesses. Every morning it puts the picture together for you: how yesterday went, what stood out, and whether to push hard today or take it easier.

How it works

There are no rigid commands to memorize — just write normally.

  • 1 You write a message. In plain language — whatever you'd naturally say to a coach.
  • 2 It figures out what you mean. An AI model (Gemini) reads the message and decides what to do — save a record, look up your history, sync a device, or search current research — there's no fixed menu it's limited to.
  • 3 It remembers you specifically. Your own goals, stats, and training plan (set once via /system_prompt) shape every answer after that.
"I weighed myself today, 88kg"
"How has my sleep been this week?"
"Sync my Garmin data from yesterday"
"I want to do Variant 1 today"

Integrations

It pulls from more than one source, so the daily picture is actually complete.

⌚

Garmin Connect

Steps, sleep, heart rate, HRV, SpO2, body battery, training readiness, and activities — synced automatically every morning, or on request.

🍽️

Nutrition diary

Connects to your kaloricketabulky.sk food log — calories, protein, carbs, fat, and the individual meals behind them.

🏋️

Workout logging

Ask for your last session of a given routine, get weight/rep suggestions based on it, and log the results back when you're done.

📚

Real science, cited

When a topic is worth grounding in research (sleep, HRV, training load...), it searches peer-reviewed studies and cites the author and year — or says plainly when it found nothing, instead of making something up.

Your morning report

Every day at 9:00, before you've even asked, it pulls together everything from the day before — training, sleep, food, anything you logged — flags anything unusual, and tells you in a couple of sentences whether today's a green light or a day to ease off. You can also just ask for it any time with /daily_report.

This is a private project, not a public product — access is by invitation only. If you don't already have access and message the bot, it'll let you know.
